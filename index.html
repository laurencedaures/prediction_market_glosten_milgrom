    Glossary

    Limit order book (LOB)
    The list of all resting limit orders, sorted by price. Buyers on one side, sellers on the other.
    Limit order
    "I will buy (or sell) at this price or better." If nobody is on the other side, it waits in the book. It supplies liquidity.
    Market order
    "Buy (or sell) right now at the best available price." It consumes liquidity and may walk through several price levels.
    Spread
    The gap between the best ask and the best bid. It is the immediate cost of a round trip.
    Price-time priority
    The best price is served first. At the same price, the earliest order is served first (a queue).
    Slippage
    The difference between the best price shown at the start and the average price a large market order actually gets.
    Aggressive / passive order
    Aggressive: it crosses the book and trades immediately. Passive: it waits for a counterparty to come to it.
    Depth
    Cumulative quantity available near the best price. A deep book absorbs large orders without moving much.
